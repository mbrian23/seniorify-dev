🏆 I won the Montevideo Zero to Agent hackathon!

As part of Vercel's global build week, I built Seniorify.dev solo and took home the local win.

Every junior now has a senior in their pocket. AI is making them incredibly fast — fluency is rising, but judgment isn't. If the AI does all the thinking, who is training the seniors of tomorrow?

The future has a senior staffing problem.

I built Seniorify.dev to fix this. It's a "senior-in-the-loop" for tools like Codex, Cursor, and Claude Code.

It kills the blind copy-paste. Seniorify.dev intercepts the PR before it's written to verify the junior actually understands what's happening, interrogating them based on the team's internal conventions:

"You chose axios, but this codebase uses @acme/internal-http. Why?"
"Invoices are in /v2/billing, not /api/users. Defend your reasoning."

It turns AI from an autocomplete tool into a Socratic tutor.

This is Programming 101 for the AI age: the code is free, but the defense is mandatory. Whether it's a student submitting an assignment or a junior hitting merge, Seniorify.dev forces them to articulate the why, not just the what.
